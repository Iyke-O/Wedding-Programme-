SS Peter & Paul Idoma Catholic community Centre, opposite Maritime Quarters, Kubwa. Abuja

Welcome

Thank you for joining us as we celebrate the beginning of our forever. We are delighted to share this special day with you.

Order of Programme

Here is the order of events for our special day.

  1. Welcome and Introduction of MC

    Guests arrive and take their seats.

  2. Entrance of Parents

    Recognition of the parents.

  3. Introduction of High Table Guests

    Introduction and recognition of High table guests.

  4. Opening Prayer

    Opening prayer and remarks

  5. Recognition of Guests

    Special recognition and appreciation of Families, friends and honoured guests.

  6. Procession (Couples Entrance)

    Entrance of newly wedded couple with grooms men and brides maids

  7. Breaking of kolanut

    Traditional presentation and breaking of kolanut.

  8. Nuptial dance by the couple

    A beautiful romantic dance by the newly married couple.

  9. MC Entertainment

    Entertainment and games by the Master of Ceremonies.

  10. Entrance of the bridal/Party

    A spectacular entrance and dance performance by bride and bridal party.

  11. Cutting of Cake

    The couple cuts their wedding cake and feed each other.

  12. Toast by the couple

    The couple gives a heartfelt toast to their guests.

  13. MC Entertainment

    Entertainment and games by the Master of Ceremonies.

  14. Couple Dance

    Special dance performance by the newly married couple.

  15. Honour Dance

    The Couple will be joined on the dance floor by.....

  16. Presentation of Gifts

    The couple receives gifts from their guests.

  17. Vote of Thanks

    The couple expresses their gratitude to guests.

  18. Closing Prayer

    The ceremony is concluded with a closing prayer.

  19. Celebration

    Dance, Dance, Dance.

Thank You
