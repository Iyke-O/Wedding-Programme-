Order of Programme

Here is the order of events for our special day.

  1. Welcome and Introduction of MC

    Guests arrive and take their seats.

  2. Entrance of Parents

    Recognition of the parents.

  3. Introduction of Chairman of the ceremony

    Introduction and recognition of High table guests.

  4. Opening Prayer

    Opening prayer and remarks

  5. Recognition of Guests

    Special recognition and appreciation of Family, friends and honoured guests.

  6. Procession

    Entrance of newly wedded couple with grooms men and brides maids

  7. Breaking of kolanut

    Traditional ritual to symbolize the couple's commitment.

  8. Nuptial dance by the couple

    A beautiful romantic dance by the newly married couple.

  9. Cutting of Cake

    The couple cuts their wedding cake and feed each other.

  10. MC Entertainment

    Entertainment and games by the Master of Ceremonies.

  11. Entrance of the bridal/Party

    A spectacular entrance and dance performance by bride and bridal party.

  12. Toast by the couple

    The couple gives a heartfelt toast to their guests.

  13. Special Song

    A musical presentation for the couple.

  14. Pronouncement of Marriage

    The couple is pronounced married.

  15. Signing of the Register

    The marriage documents are signed.

  16. Cutting of the Cake

    The couple cuts their wedding cake.
